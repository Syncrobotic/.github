# Security policy

Our software runs robots and cameras in real buildings, so we treat security reports as urgent. Thank you for helping keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.** Public disclosure
before a fix puts users at risk.

Report privately through either channel:

1. **GitHub private advisory** (preferred). On the affected repository, go to
   **Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability**. This opens a private
   thread with the maintainers.
2. **Email** [security@syncrobotic.com](mailto:security@syncrobotic.com) with
   the details below.

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce (proof-of-concept if possible).
- Affected repository, version/commit, and environment.
- Any suggested remediation.

## What to expect

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement of your report | within **3 business days** |
| Initial assessment & severity triage | within **7 business days** |
| Fix or mitigation plan | communicated after triage, based on severity |

We'll keep you updated along the way and, if you like, credit you once it's fixed.

## Responsible disclosure

- Give us reasonable time to investigate and remediate before any public disclosure.
- Do not access, modify, or delete data that isn't yours, and avoid privacy
  violations, service degradation, or data destruction while testing.
- Act in good faith. We won't pursue action against researchers who follow this policy.

## Scope

This policy covers repositories under the [`Syncrobotic`](https://github.com/Syncrobotic)
organization. For anything else, email us and we'll route it.
